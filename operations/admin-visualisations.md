# Admin visualisations

In the new admin, press **g**, then **g** to cycle between hidden artwork, the
space station, and the village. The preference is saved in a browser cookie.
The header represents resources belonging to the selected company. Each company
member adds a worker. Applications and stacks with larger RAM/CPU plans or
profiles have larger modules or houses.

While the station or village is visible, two buttons appear before Keyboard
shortcuts. **City** opens a full-screen city in a new tab, leaving the admin
open. **Galaxy** opens the existing `/galaxy` page in another tab. Both buttons
are hidden when the header artwork is hidden.

The city requires an admin login and uses the company selected when opened.
Each application, stack, Page, registered domain, and DNS zone has one building
and contributes five people and five cars. A registered domain that also has a
DNS zone contributes two buildings. Application and stack building sizes follow
their RAM and CPU capacity; Pages, domains, and zones have similar small sizes.

Select a category to highlight it, or hover/tap a building for details. Use
**Refresh city** after changing services, **Pause animation** or Space to stop
traffic, and the full-screen button to hide browser chrome. Reduced-motion
preferences start the city paused. No resource edits are made by the city.

The city is private company data from the existing authenticated APIs. It is
separate from the public galaxy visualisation and does not publish account data.
